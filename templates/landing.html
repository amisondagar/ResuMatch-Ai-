Features

Everything You Need to Hire Better

From resume parsing to AI-generated interview questions — all in one platform.

AI Resume Parsing
Automatically extracts name, email, skills, education, experience, projects, and certifications from PDF and DOCX resumes.
ATS Score Calculation
Smart scoring using skill matching (40%), keyword match (30%), experience (20%), and education (10%) — transparent and explainable.
Candidate Ranking
Automatically rank all candidates from highest to lowest match score. Shortlist top talent with one click.
Rich Analytics
Interactive Plotly charts showing ATS distribution, top skills, hiring ratio, and hiring trends over time.
Interview Questions
AI-generated interview questions tailored to each candidate's matched skills — save hours of interview prep.
PDF & CSV Reports
Download professional PDF reports for individual candidates or ranked lists. Export to CSV for further analysis.
Side-by-Side Compare
Compare two candidates head-to-head across all score dimensions to make the best hiring decision.
Duplicate Detection
Automatically detect duplicate resume uploads using MD5 file hashing — never process the same resume twice.
Dark & Light Mode
Beautiful premium UI with glassmorphism design, smooth animations, and full dark/light mode support.

How ResumeMatch.ai Works

Four simple steps from upload to shortlist.

1
Upload Resumes
Drag & drop single or multiple PDF/DOCX resumes. Bulk upload supported.
2
Paste Job Description
Paste or upload your JD. Our AI extracts required skills and experience automatically.
3
AI Analyzes & Scores
Our AI engine runs semantic matching, skill comparison, and generates ATS scores for all candidates.
4
Shortlist & Report
View rankings, generate reports, add notes, tags, and export your shortlist as PDF or CSV.
